INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
V3 estimates a historical within-grid response rather than directly perturbing the frozen LightGBM prediction model.
Grid fixed effects remove time-invariant location differences; year fixed effects remove Kaduwela-wide annual shocks.
This strengthens intervention interpretation, but observational data still do not prove causality.
Positive cooling_benefit_c means the estimated historical green response lowers LST relative to the Step-28 baseline.
Response-model 95% CI is not total forecast uncertainty. Step 30 will handle future prediction uncertainty.

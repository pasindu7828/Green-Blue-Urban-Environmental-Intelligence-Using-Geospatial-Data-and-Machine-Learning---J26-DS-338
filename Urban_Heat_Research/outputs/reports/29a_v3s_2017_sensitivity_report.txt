INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2017 remains in the primary dataset and V3 primary result. V3S is only a robustness/sensitivity analysis.
No coefficient is automatically preferred based on whether it produces cooling or warming.
If the positive coefficient remains stable without 2017 and across leave-one-year-out / first-difference checks, then 2017 alone does not explain the positive association.
If the sign or magnitude changes strongly when 2017 is removed, the final scenario interpretation must explicitly report that sensitivity.
Observational panel associations are not causal proof.

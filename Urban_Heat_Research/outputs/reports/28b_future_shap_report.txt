INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Positive centered SHAP means a feature pushes that grid hotter relative to Kaduwela's annual thermal level.
Negative centered SHAP means a feature pushes that grid cooler relative to Kaduwela's annual thermal level.
SHAP explains only the spatial-anomaly LightGBM branch. It does not explain the separate annual-level forecast.
These are model attributions, not causal effects.
Future SHAP uses forecast predictor values, so its interpretation inherits uncertainty from Step-28 future predictor forecasting.

STEP 29A - GREEN-COVER WHAT-IF COOLING SCENARIO SIMULATION
======================================================================================================================

SCENARIO DESIGN
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary: +5 percentage points green cover. Sensitivity levels: +10 pp and +20 pp.
Each grid-year is an independent local intervention; the batch is not a simultaneous citywide greening scenario.

MODEL POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The frozen Step-22B LightGBM is used without retraining. Step-28 baseline forecasts are not modified.
Scenario LST = Step-28 baseline LST + (scenario raw anomaly - baseline raw anomaly).
Cooling benefit = baseline LST - scenario LST. Positive values indicate modeled cooling.

MONOTONICITY DIAGNOSTIC
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16.14% of grid-year cases show non-decreasing modeled cooling from +5 to +10 to +20 pp.
Non-monotonic cases are retained; tree-model monotonicity is not forced.

INTERPRETATION LIMITATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These outputs represent modeled statistical sensitivity, not guaranteed physical cooling outcomes.
Real cooling depends on vegetation species, canopy geometry, placement, irrigation, maintenance, land conversion, and other factors not explicitly represented.
Uncertainty/confidence is not added in Step 29; it is handled in Step 30.

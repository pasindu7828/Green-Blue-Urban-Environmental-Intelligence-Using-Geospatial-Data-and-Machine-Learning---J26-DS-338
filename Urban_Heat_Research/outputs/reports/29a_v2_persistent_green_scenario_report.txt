STEP 29A V2 - PERSISTENT GREEN-COVER WHAT-IF COOLING SCENARIO
======================================================================================================================

WHY V2
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
V2 applies the green-cover intervention from 2026 onward and propagates scenario values into future lag/history features.
This is temporally more coherent than the V1 direct same-year perturbation diagnostic.

SCENARIO DESIGN
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary +5 percentage points; sensitivity +10 and +20 percentage points; values clipped to [0,1].
Each grid is an independent local intervention. Neighbouring grids remain at their Step-28 baseline future state.

INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Positive cooling_benefit_c means the persistent scenario is cooler than the same grid-year Step-28 baseline.
Negative cooling_benefit_c means the predictive model associates the scenario with warming for that grid-year.
Results are statistical model sensitivity, not causal or guaranteed real-world cooling.
If persistent scenarios still overwhelmingly predict warming, the frozen predictive LightGBM should not be presented as a green-intervention cooling estimator.

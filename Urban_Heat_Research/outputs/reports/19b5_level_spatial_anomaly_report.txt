STEP 19B5 - CAUSAL ANNUAL-LEVEL + SPATIAL-ANOMALY DECOMPOSITION
====================================================================================================================

PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Test whether weak temporal generalization is driven mainly by difficulty forecasting the annual thermal level while spatial heat pattern remains learnable.

FORMULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LST(grid, year) = annual thermal level(year) + spatial anomaly(grid, year)

DATA POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Development rows: 2018-2023 only.
2024 reserved validation: NOT loaded.
2025 final lockbox: NOT loaded.

ANNUAL LEVEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eligible level forecasts use prior annual target means only.
Rules: persistence, prior-mean, and simple OLS trend.
Linear trend requires at least 3 prior annual years; otherwise it falls back to persistence.

SPATIAL ANOMALY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Random Forest and LightGBM reuse frozen Step 17 hyperparameters.
Training anomaly targets are centered separately inside each historical training year.
Validation predicted anomalies are re-centered using prediction mean only.

INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If anomaly R² stays positive while causal final-LST R² remains weak because annual-level forecast error is large, annual thermal level is supported as a major operational bottleneck.
If oracle-level predictions are strong but causal level forecasts are weak, the decomposition is informative but does not yet solve future absolute-LST forecasting.
If anomaly skill itself is weak, the annual-level hypothesis alone is insufficient.

NO FINAL MODEL SELECTION

Step 19B5 remains development evidence only. Step 19C must compare legitimate Step 19 strategies before 2024.

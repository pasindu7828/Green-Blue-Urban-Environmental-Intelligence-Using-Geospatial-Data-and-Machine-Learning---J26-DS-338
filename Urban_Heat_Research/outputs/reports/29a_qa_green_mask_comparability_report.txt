INTERPRETATION RULE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If green_mask and NDVI/EVI give conflicting longitudinal response signs, do not treat Otsu green percentage as a validated numeric cooling intervention variable without resolving the measurement definition.
If all vegetation indicators show the same robust positive within-grid association, the dataset itself does not support a numeric vegetation-cooling response estimate under these observational specifications.
If green-only and fully controlled green models reverse sign, interpret the positive V3 coefficient as control-sensitive/suppression-affected rather than as a simple physical green effect.
No result is causal proof.

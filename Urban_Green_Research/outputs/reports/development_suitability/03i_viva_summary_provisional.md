## Important limitations

The AHP matrix contains four cross-study adapted comparisons and two researcher scenario assumptions.

This is not a genuine Kaduwela expert assessment and does not yet meet the approved expert-AHP requirement.

The map does not establish legal development permission, land availability or independent development-suitability accuracy.

All results remain PROVISIONAL pending expert review or an approved methodology change.
